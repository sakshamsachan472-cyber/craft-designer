ABOUT THE SHOES

Shoes are inspired by Warli art and culture.

Warli art is a tribal folk art form from

Maharashtra, India, created by the Warli people

One of the oldest styles of Indian art, dating back thousands of years

known for its simple yet expressive geometric patterns.

Traditionally, Warli paintings were done on mud walls using rice paste,

they depict scenes of daily life, nature, and rituals.

✨ Key Features of Warli Art

Geometric Shapes: Circles, triangles, and squares are used to represent humans, animals, and natural elements.

Storytelling: Often shows farming, dancing, hunting, or community life.

Minimalist Style: Simple lines and forms, yet deeply symbolic.

Cultural Roots: Reflects harmony with nature and the importance of community.

✨ Meaning of the Women’s Circle

The women’s circle motif is inspired by Warli art,

where tribal women are shown dancing together in harmony.

It symbolizes unity, celebration, and the timeless bond between community and nature —

a heritage story reimagined on sneakers.

✨ Meaning of “Home” in Warli Art

The home motif in Warli art is more than a hut

it symbolizes belonging, safety, and the heartbeat of community life

Paired with the women’s circle, it tells a story of unity

tradition, and the joy of living together.
