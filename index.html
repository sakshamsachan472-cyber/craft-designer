About the Warli Art Shoes

Shoes Inspired by Warli Art and Culture

Warli Art is a tribal folk art form from Maharashtra, India, created by the Warli people.

One of the oldest styles of Indian art, dating back thousands of years

Known for its simple yet expressive geometric patterns.

Traditionally, Warli paintings were created on mud walls using rice paste, depicting scenes of daily life, nature, and community rituals.

View Product

✨ Key Features of Warli Art

Geometric Shapes: Circles, triangles, and squares represent humans, animals, and natural elements.

Storytelling: Shows farming, dancing, hunting, and community life.

Minimalist Style: Simple lines and forms with deep symbolic meaning.

Cultural Roots: Reflects harmony with nature and community values.

✨ Women's Circle Motif Meaning

The women's circle is inspired by Warli art, where tribal women dance together in harmony.

It symbolizes unity, celebration, and timeless community bonds.

Heritage tradition reimagined on contemporary sneakers.

✨ "Home" Symbol in Warli Art

The home motif represents more than shelter—it symbolizes belonging, safety, and community heartbeat.

Paired with the women's circle, it tells a story of unity, tradition, and joyful living together.
